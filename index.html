Waste Generation & Reduction

The most effective waste-management step often happens before anything reaches a bin. Better product choices, responsible consumption, reuse and repair reduce the waste requiring collection and treatment.

Why it matters

Less waste generated means lower collection demand, fewer discarded materials and more resources kept in circulation.
