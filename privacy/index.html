What this prototype does—and doesn't do.

This page explains the limits of a static demonstration created for theme and UX review.

A preview, not a live business system.

This site is a public GitHub Pages design scaffold. It contains no accounts, login, tracking pixels, payment features or enquiry-storage backend. The learning simulator and quizzes run in your browser and do not submit your answers to a server.

Fonts may be fetched from Google Fonts if your browser permits them. External links, including the official GeoCirculus site, have their own data practices.

Content draws on the client's October 2026 pitch deck and educational references. Proposed pilot operations, forecast outcomes, availability and leadership details require validation before being treated as definitive corporate statements.
