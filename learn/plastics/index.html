Material science

A useful material. A difficult waste stream.

Plastics are a diverse family of polymer-based materials valued for durability, low weight and versatility. Those advantages become challenges when incompatible or contaminated materials are mixed.

Identify. Sort. Preserve material value.
Five relevant polymer families

Recognize the basics.

1

PET

Common in beverage bottles. Sorting and washing can produce reusable flakes under suitable conditions.

2

HDPE

Found in rigid bottles, drums and containers. Can be reprocessed when properly separated.

4

LDPE

Common in flexible films. Contamination and thin-film handling can make processing demanding.

5

PP

Used in bags, packaging and household products. Typically requires controlled separation.

3

PVC

Used in pipes and cables. Requires a dedicated processing stream, not mixing with most other plastics.

!

A critical caution

Polymer codes do not guarantee local recyclability; always consider collection and processing facilities.
