Proposed recycling pilot

The Smart Recycling Node.

The proposed 1.5–2 tonnes-per-day plastic recovery pilot is designed around sorting, washing, shredding and granulation. At 26 working days, its target processing range would be 39–52 tonnes/month.

These figures describe proposed capacity rather than measured output.
