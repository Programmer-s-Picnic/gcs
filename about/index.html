About

Progress through circularity.

GeoCirculus Ecotech Pvt Ltd aims to link scientific recycling, digital intelligence and inclusive livelihoods into a practical circular-economy ecosystem.

Vision

Prosperity in every cycle.

“Nothing Wasted. Everything Circulated. Prosperity Generated.”

Transform waste into opportunity, innovation into impact, and circularity into shared prosperity, beginning with the needs of India's heritage cities.

North-star thinking
∞
Cleaner cities. Resilient communities. Resource stewardship.
Our mission

One connected ecosystem.

Six themes from the company mission form the foundation of the proposed digital experience.

♻

Scientific recovery

Technology-enabled collection, sorting, recycling and material recovery.

⌖

Geospatial insight

Digital monitoring, mapped collection and planned batch traceability.

♧

Inclusive livelihoods

Skill-building, SHG participation and waste-worker inclusion.

▤

Business responsibility

Helping partners navigate EPR, ESG and circular-economy opportunities.

≈

Heritage stewardship

Beginning in Varanasi with respect for cultural and environmental contexts.

↗

Scalable innovation

Validate supply, quality, economics and traceability in a pilot before scaling.
